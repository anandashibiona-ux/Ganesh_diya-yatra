# Diya Yatra — Improved Edition

## New start experience
- `ganesh.mp4` is used as the cinematic opening screen.
- The video starts automatically (muted + inline for browser autoplay compatibility).
- After 5 seconds, **Tap to Begin** appears on top of the video.
- Clicking anywhere on the intro after the button appears opens the **Game Center**.

## Game Center
There are 5 roads with increasing difficulty:
1. Temple Approach — Calm
2. Lamp Valley — Warm
3. Serpent Ridge — Fiery
4. Ghat Climb — Steep
5. Summit of Light — Master

Roads unlock progressively after reaching the distance requirement of the previous road. Progress is saved in local storage.

## Visual / smoothness improvements
- More readable road ribbon and edge lighting.
- Smoother camera interpolation.
- Frame-rate independent physics with a tighter delta-time cap.
- Additional festival embers / fireflies for depth.
- Stronger HUD glass effect and motion polish.
- Level-aware terrain amplitude, roughness, gravity, fuel drain and tilt limits.
- Road Map access from the results screen and replay of the currently selected road.
